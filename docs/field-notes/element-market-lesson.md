# Field note: "No market on the project site" ≠ "no market"

**Situation.** A BSC Agent-ID collection opened a cheap paid mint. The
project site linked no marketplace, and the mint was initially framed as a
red flag and skipped.

**What was actually true.** Element Market had indexed the collection
permissionlessly right after minting opened. A real floor formed
(~3× the mint price) with sales clearing at ~2–3× within the first hour.

**Lesson.**
- For every Agent-ID project, check Element Market **before** minting and
  again **within the first hour** after mint opens — whether or not the
  project links it.
- For a cheap mint whose *only* flags are "contract not published" or
  "reward token not launched yet" — with no fund-moving payload, no
  approval/drain pattern — present the numbers neutrally (cost, worst case =
  lose the mint price, upside if a floor forms) and let a human decide.
- True danger signs (approvals, transfers, unauditable fund movements)
  still hard-block. Soft flags are pricing information, not verdicts.
